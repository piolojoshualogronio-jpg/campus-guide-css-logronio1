Eating Well on a Student Budget

Your first year is busy: new classes, new friends, and long days on campus. It is easy to skip meals or spend your whole allowance on milk tea. Eatingregular, simple meals will help you focus in class , and you do not need a big budget to do it.

Where to Eat Near Campus

You have more options than you think. Here are the usual go-to spots

  • The school canteen for quick rice meals between classes
  • Carinderias outside the gate for cheap, filling ulam and rice
  • Your own baon from home, which is the cheapest choice of all
  • convinience stores for emergency snacks, not for every meals
